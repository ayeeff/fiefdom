☢️ Wave 4: The Unsuitable Frontier

Radioactive by nature: All actinides are unstable, emitting ionizing radiation that requires extreme containment.

Gram-scale existence: Most exist only in trace amounts or must be synthesized. No industrial mining possible.

Proliferation risk: Dual-use concerns make widespread adoption politically impossible.

The broken wave: Unlike Waves 1-3, these materials cannot power mass-market technology—only niche, high-risk applications.
