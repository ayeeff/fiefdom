The Full Stack: US Complexity vs China Uniformity (with Cost Estimates)

Expanding the view to the metal—and adding the numbers. The US spends fragmented billions across clouds ($9B JWCC), chips ($3.5B Intel), licenses ($100M+ Oracle/SAP), networks (DISA/SIPRNet), middleware chaos, and a $10B Palantir integration layer just to make it work. China achieves end-to-end control with state-subsidized domestic tech: Inspur, Huawei, Kylin, Dameng—all vertically integrated under military-civil fusion at lower total cost.

💡 The Palantir Problem

Palantir is the symptom, not the solution. The US needs a $10B integration layer because:

  • Army runs SAP on Windows with Custom ESB over DISA
  • Navy runs SAP on Linux with MuleSoft over SIPRNet
  • Air Force splits between Azure/AWS with IBM MQ and different middleware

China built analytics into their unified stack from day one. They don't need a Palantir equivalent because there's nothing to integrate—it's all one pipeline.
