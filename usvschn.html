The Full Stack: US Complexity vs China Uniformity

Expanding the view to the metal—and the wires between. The US manages a chaotic mix of commercial clouds (AWS/Azure), diverse chips (Intel/AMD), competing OS/DB standards, fragmented networks (DISA/SIPRNet/Commercial), and incompatible middleware. China has consolidated the entire vertical: from the sand (Chips) through unified networks to a single integration layer, all managed by JLSF.
