📊 Market Briefing: The $1.2T Surplus
The Headline
China Defies US Tariffs with Record Surplus
Despite aggressive US tariffs, China is on track for a $1.2 Trillion trade surplus in 2025. US efforts to curb exports have failed to reduce China's global dominance.
The Strategy
The "Global South" Pivot
Blocked from the US, China flooded new markets. Exports to India, Africa, and Southeast Asia are breaking records. Manufacturers are rerouting goods through third-party countries to dodge Western tariffs.
The Backlash
Global Trade War Expands
It's no longer just the US. Mexico imposed 50% tariffs on Chinese steel/EVs. India, Indonesia, and Brazil have launched anti-dumping investigations to protect local industries from cheap imports.
The Risk
"Profitless Growth" Trap
The boom hides a crisis. Industrial profits are down 2% as factories slash prices to move excess supply. This is fueling a domestic deflationary spiral despite high export volumes.
